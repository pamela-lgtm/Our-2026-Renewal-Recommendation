Build-to-Rent Communities Are Competing for Your Tenant
Single-family builders are actively delivering brand-new homes into build-to-rent (BTR) communities across Central Florida — purpose-built single-family homes offered for rent, often with upgraded finishes and amenities. Large institutional investors and major homebuilders have launched dedicated BTR divisions throughout the region, including right here in Lake Nona. Your tenant has more never-before-lived-in single-family options to compare against your property than ever before.
🏠
The Rise of the "Accidental Landlord"
With mortgage rates hovering at 6–7%, many homeowners who expected to sell simply cannot. Rather than take a loss or sit vacant, they're converting those homes to rentals — adding inventory to an already competitive market and directly competing with your property.
💸
Inflation Has Squeezed Tenants
The post-pandemic rent spikes were extraordinary — some Florida landlords saw 20%+ annual increases between 2020 and 2023. But wages didn't keep pace. Today's tenants are budget-conscious and comparison-shopping online in real time. A rent even slightly above market means a longer vacancy.
🤝
Tenants Are Doubling Up — or Opting Out
Affordability pressure has led many renters to share households or downsize rather than renew at higher rates. The renter pool for single-family homes at premium price points has genuinely shrunk. Good tenants who pay on time are harder to replace than ever.
📉
The Correction Is Market-Wide, Not Just Orlando
This isn't a local blip. Jacksonville led the nation with a 12% year-over-year rent drop by mid-2024. Tampa saw rents fall roughly 6% in the same window — following a 38% spike in 2022. Orlando's trajectory mirrors the broader Sun Belt correction as the frenzied pandemic-era market normalizes.

What Happens Next

Our Renewal Process From Here

We begin this process 2½ to 3 months before your lease end date to ensure nothing is rushed and everything is compliant with Florida statutes.

Now — Renewal Rate Determined
We've reviewed current market conditions and your tenant's payment history. Our recommendation is renewal at the current rate — protecting your investment and your tenant relationship in this correcting market.
Next — Renewal Offer to Tenant
Within the next week, we will present the renewal offer to your tenant. Under Florida statutes, tenants requiring 60-day notice must have their renewal decision finalized no later than 65 days before lease expiration.
If Needed — We Negotiate on Your Behalf
If there is any pushback or counter from the tenant, we handle those negotiations strategically — always with your investment's long-term stability as the priority.
Final — Signed Renewal Delivered to You
Once we have a fully executed lease renewal, we'll send you a copy and upload it to your Owner Portal. If the tenant decides not to renew, we notify you immediately and begin our re-leasing process.

The post-pandemic rent boom was real, and your investment benefited from it. But the market is correcting — and the owners who will come out ahead in this cycle are the ones who hold their good tenants close, not the ones chasing an extra $50 or $100 a month and ending up with 60 days of vacancy in return.

We are not passive managers, and this recommendation is not a passive one. It is a deliberate, data-informed strategy built on 20+ years of experience in this market — and we are confident it is the right call.
